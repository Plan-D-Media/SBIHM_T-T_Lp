Travel & Tourism Management — The 4-Year Journey

Duration 4 Years (8 Semesters)
Eligibility 10+2 passed/appeared from a recognised Central or State Govt. Board
Affiliation MAKAUT
Intake 60 seats per campus
1

Year 1 — Foundation

Tourism Principles & Practices • Principles of Marketing • Computer Fundamentals • Communicative English • Tourism Geography • Business Communication

2

Year 2 — Core Skills + Industrial Training

Transport in Travel & Tourism • Travel Agency & Tour Operation • International Tourism • Tourism Product • Understanding Hotels • Basic Accounting • Principles of Management. Plus a mandatory 6-month industrial training placement in a 5-star deluxe hotel — real operations, real responsibility.

3

Year 3 — Specialisation

Tourism Economics • Eco Tourism & Sustainable Development • Tourism Planning & Policy • Tourism Marketing • Organisational Behaviour & HRM in Tourism • Financial Management • Legal & Ethical Issues in Tourism • Entrepreneurship Development in Tourism. Plus placement preparation — interview skills, industry interactions, on-campus and off-campus placements with travel agencies, tour operators, airlines, hotels and resorts.

4

Year 4 — Specialisation & Placement

Tourism Marketing • Tourism Geography • Legal & Ethical Issues in Tourism • Entrepreneurship Development in Tourism. Plus placement preparation — mock GDs, interview skills, industry interactions, and on-campus & off-campus placements with travel agencies, tour operators, airlines, hotels and resorts.
